# Level 8, THE SNOWY MOUNTAIN (2026-10-19)

Story level 8 built as its own level, like levels 1–6: rules in
`src/config/levels/snow.ts`, looks in `src/renderDoodle/levels/snow.ts`,
creature rules in `src/objects/snowTraits.ts`, the boss in
`src/objects/yeti.ts`, a test in `src/sim/snow.test.ts`. Design and bot
numbers: `docs/SOLO_MODES_DESIGN.md` "Level 8".

The idea that ties the page together: on a snowy slope everything slides,
rolls and gathers speed, so every creature says "answer me early", each in
its own way (a jump closer, one more number, more speed).

Subgoals, in the order the change was built:
1. a sum that grows (the snowball): `SpeciesDef.BOX` + `AbilityHost.addBall`;
2. the penguin, ice block and snowflake as traits on `pace`;
3. the Yeti as a Boss subclass that throws minions (`spawnMinion`);
4. the doodles;
5. tune with bots.

### 1. A sum that grows: a box planned ahead, and `addBall`
**What changed:** a snowball that rolls past y 250 gets one more number:
its two numbers stay and a third is added, so 7 + 5 becomes 7 + 5 + 3.
`Swarm.addBall` picks the new digit so the answer is still unique on the
field. The snowball's readability box is sized for three balls from the
start (`SpeciesDef.BOX`).
**Pattern:** invariant fixed at construction. `Alien` computes its box once,
in its constructor (`boxFor`), and every readability check trusts it. A
value like that can't follow later changes, so a creature that will grow
must be built at its biggest size.
**Why this way:** if the box grew when the third ball arrived, it could
grow into a neighbor's box with nobody checking, and numbers would overlap
(the one thing the readability rule forbids). A box that already fits the
last ball makes growing always safe.
**Alternatives considered:**
- Re-roll the sum with three balls (like the dragon's breath) — lost: it
  throws away the work a player already did. Adding a ball keeps it, which
  is fairer and teaches that a sum can be built on.
- Let the box change and re-check readability when it grows — lost: what
  happens if it doesn't fit? Wait? Skip growing? Each answer is new code in
  `Swarm`, shared by every level, while three other levels are being built.
- The "cluster" layout (rows of two) — lost: its box grows upward with each
  new row, the same problem.
**How to change it later:** `SNOWBALL_GROW` in `config/levels/snow.ts`
(where it grows, how slow it rolls afterwards). Another creature that grows
needs a `BOX` that fits its last ball and calls `host.addBall?.(alien)`.

### 2. Penguins, ice blocks, snowflakes: three uses of one hook
**What changed:** three traits, each overriding `Ability.pace` (the cave's
hook: how many ms of its kind's own movement the alien runs this step).
The penguin counts its waddle time and then holds still (the lean, its
telegraph) and glides 70 px (the slide). The ice block returns more time
the lower it is (`slopeProgress`), so it speeds up. The snowflake switches
between a calm pace and a gust pace on its own clock.
**Pattern:** hook with a default (template method, ARCHITECTURE.md §2.7).
**Why this way:** `pace` runs on field time and only while no glide or hold
is running, so FREEZE and SLOW hold every rhythm, and penguins in a line
(spawned together, same clocks) slide together for free.
**Alternatives considered:**
- `Ability.update` (real time) for the penguin's timer — lost: a FREEZE
  would still count the waddle, and the penguin would slide right after it.
- `SpeciesDef.SPEED` alone for the ice block — lost: it's one number for
  the whole trip, and the point is a speed that changes with position.
**How to change it later:** `PENGUIN_SLIDE`, `ICE_SLIDE`, `SNOWFLAKE_GUST`
in `config/levels/snow.ts`.

### 3. The Yeti throws snowballs: an optional host method
**What changed:** the Yeti (a `Boss` subclass, like the dragon) walks 45 px,
raises its fists (the strafer's "windup" mode, which the view shakes and
bots read as danger), then stomps and throws a snowball to the roomier side
with `host.spawnMinion?.("swooper", from, to, ms)`. The minion is the
level's own creature for that kind, so it is a real snowball that grows.
**Pattern:** dependency inversion through a small interface, grown with an
**optional method** (ARCHITECTURE.md §2.8). `addBall?` and `spawnMinion?`
end with `?`, and callers write `host.addBall?.(alien)`.
**Why this way:** `swamp.test.ts` (another level's file) builds a stand-in
`AbilityHost` with three methods. Required new methods would break it, and
the other levels' branches may have their own stand-ins. Optional methods
keep every existing implementation compiling.
**Alternatives considered:**
- `spawnBossPart` with an HP-1 "snowball boss" — lost: boss parts dig back
  up after reaching the ship and must all be beaten to clear the level.
- `spawnSplitling` — lost: it always makes a pink splitling, never the
  page's creature.
- Required methods + editing the swamp test — lost: it touches another
  level's file and could break branches still being built.
**How to change it later:** `YETI_STOMP` (walk, windup, how many of its
snowballs may be up). A boss that calls a different creature passes another
kind to `spawnMinion`.

### 4. The doodles
Same shape as every page: a `PageDef` with props, one `CreatureLook` per
species (its `variant` picks a pre-drawn picture per state: penguin
waddle/warn/slide, snowball size, ice slow/fast, snowflake calm/gust) and a
`BossLook` with `under` for the stomp rings. Snow is slate, frost grey and
teal, never blue: blue is the sum balls' color.

### 5. Tuning by bots
First try: pilots cleared 0/12, with grown snowballs and ice blocks causing
nearly every hit. Snowballs at ×0.75 that grow at y 250 and roll at half
pace afterwards, and ice blocks at 0.4× → 1.0×, gave rookie 0/20, pilot
6/20, ace 20/20 (2.5 lives left). The volcano on the same script: pilot 9/12.

**Verified by:** `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm test` (129 passed, golden master
unchanged), `npm run build`; played in the browser with `?bot=ace` (a full
clear, then the bot switched off to watch the Yeti stomp and throw).
